About Me

My academic background is in Physics and Astronomy, with a strong focus on computational science. I completed my undergraduate studies at Valdosta State University and earned my Master's degree in Physics at Georgia Tech. During my research internship at Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory, I worked on molecular dynamics simulations studying deuterium diffusion in boron. My interests include computational physics, plasma physics, gravity, active matter, numerical modeling, and scientific computing.

Research & Projects

Selected research experiences and computational projects from my academic and scientific work.

Plasma Physics · Summer 2025

Boron–Deuterium Diffusion

At Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory, I studied deuterium diffusion in alpha-rhombohedral boron using molecular dynamics simulations. The project involved LAMMPS, interatomic potentials, and computational analysis.

Active Matter · 2026

Suspended Animation

Computational study of active biological patterns and their survival under reduced energy conditions. The project combines active matter, phase separation, and glass physics.

Computational Physics

Three-Body Problem

Numerical investigation of gravitational interactions in three-body systems. Implemented numerical integration using Python and the DOP853 solver.

Quantum Information

Quantum Phase Estimation

Simulation of quantum phase estimation under imperfect gates, comparing standard and approximate quantum Fourier transforms.
