About Me

I am a physicist with a background in computational physics and astronomy. My academic and research experience has focused on numerical methods, scientific computing, simulations, and the study of complex physical systems. I completed my M.S. in Physics at the Georgia Institute of Technology and my undergraduate studies in Physics and Astronomy at Valdosta State University. I have also gained research experience at the Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory, where I worked on molecular dynamics simulations involving boron and deuterium.

Research & Projects

Plasma Physics Research

Suspended Animation

Three Body Problem

Quantum Phase Estimation
